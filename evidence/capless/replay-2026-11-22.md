# Capless entry: 17-day replay at 1k, 10k and 100k USDC (otto/mamoru#127)

**Dataset.** Base, every registry pool, one sample every 1,500 blocks (300 s at Base's 200 ms blocks). 4,847 samples
from block 53,557,500 (2026-11-05 18:02 UTC, when Base moved to 200 ms, otto/mamoru#445) to 2026-11-22. Built with
`packages/backtest/cli/backfill.ts --from-block 53557500 --step 1500` from public nodes (no operator key). Dataset id
`0x3eab8f8163a4e40755642899a9bea5902bb8049529f56d40b4405e227885f808`.

**Runs.** `packages/backtest/cli/capless-replay.ts`: the live policies v7 and v8, prod engine settings (depth sizing
30/500/5), the cap set to the deposit (no cap), a review every sample, sessions enforced.

**What it shows for #127**
- **No refusal by size:** 0 refused and 0 discarded at every size. Each deposit is placed in slices sized to depth,
  and the number of entry swaps grows with the deposit (v8: 37, 194, 591).
- **Placement scales:** fees grow about 10× from 1k to 10k and again from 10k to 100k. At 100k, depth slows the entry
  (more slices) but does not stop it.
- **Every idle amount has a reason:** each review that does not act names its wait code.

**What it does not show**
- **Yield.** The window is a falling market (holding the mix lost about 5 %). Net results track it, and LP vs hold is
  negative from impermanent loss.
- **Vault buckets.** The dataset holds pools only, so the replay cannot price vault liquidity. Vault buckets wait
  (`DECIDE_WAIT_VAULT_LIQUIDITY` in most reviews) instead of depositing. v8's 50 % stable share is therefore under-placed
  here; vault depth comes from the vault's own liquidity, not from a pool.
- **30 days.** A 30-day window would straddle Base's block-time change. The backfill's day count assumed 2 s blocks
  until #445, so this replay starts at the change: 16.8 days.

A bad read fixed on the way: two samples whose oracle cumulatives disagreed (rotating public providers) gave a TWAP
tick of -482 billion. `twapTick` now falls back to the spot tick for a mean outside ±887,272
