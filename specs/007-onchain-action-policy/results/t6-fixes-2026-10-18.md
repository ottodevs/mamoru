## H-1: measurement

All numbers at Base block 52,700,000, `test/H1Measure.t.sol` (`forge test --mc H1Measure -vv`). "Capital" is what
the attacker sends in; it comes back on the revert swap, less fees. A flash loan cannot span a block boundary, so
the capital is the attacker's own.

Windows 1,800 s and 300 s. Harmonic-mean liquidity (HM) as a share of its value before (2.972e14 for both).

| 100 | 119.8k | 23.9 | 1 | 1 | 14.2% | 2.7% |
| 500 | 120.8k | 24.1 | 1 | 4 | 12.5% | 2.3% |
| 1,000 | 121.2k | 24.2 | 2 | 7 | 4.7% | 0.8% |

**H-1 is confirmed**: 25 USDC of fees moves the 30-minute TWAP by the whole stables band. Held across N blocks
(the fork has no arbitrageurs, so holding is free here): 9,000 ticks for 2 / 5 / 15 / 45 blocks shifts the long

attacker gives away if someone else reverts the push first is 27 USDC at 10 ticks, 138 at 100, 2,598 at 9,000
(`test_h1_arbGiveaway`). Whether the attacker can be last in N and first in N+1 on Base (priority-fee ordering,

### What stops it

A deviation of D ticks held for t seconds moves a W-second TWAP by D t / W. The policy reads two windows (1,800 s
and 300 s) and prices from `lo = max(long, short) - band + 1`, `hi = min(long, short) + band`, refusing when that is
empty (the TWAPs about 2 x band apart).

- **A recent push** (inside the last 300 s) moves the short TWAP 6x more than the long one: the 9,000-tick block
  moves them 60 and 10 ticks; on stables (band 10) the range is empty, on btc a 90,000-tick block (long 100, short
  600) empties it too. Refused: `test_H1_oneBlockOutlierOnStablesIsRefused`, `test_H1_oneBlockOutlierOnBtcIsRefused`.
- **An old push** (300-1,800 s ago) moves only the long TWAP. For a token0 sale the floor is `max(long, short)`, the
  unpushed short one; for a purchase the ceiling only drops. `test_H1_oldPushAloneDoesNotLoosenTheRange`.
- **What gets through: one old and one recent push, sized about 5:1**, so both TWAPs move together. Measured
  (`test_h1_spreadPushOnBtc`):

  | USDC/cbBTC pushes (old / recent, ticks) | Own capital (old / recent) | Fees | Long / short shift | Floor moved |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | 75,000 / 15,000 | 12.1M / 4.7M | 12.5k | 102 / 101 | 102 ticks |
  | 60,000 / 30,000 | 5.2M / 4.8M | 9.1k | 102 / 201 | 102 ticks |
  | 45,000 / 15,000 | 4.9M / 4.7M | 8.9k | 69 / 101 | 69 ticks |

  So about **9k of fees and ~10M of the attacker's own capital, held across two block boundaries it must win,
  move the btc range by 1%**, for at most 5 minutes (until the recent push leaves the short window; each further
  5 minutes needs another recent push, ~4.4k). On USDC/USDT the same two pushes cost ~25 USDC each.

That last case is why the stables pool also gets a **fixed anchor**: its range is clipped to parity +- 40 ticks
(USDC and USDT both have 6 decimals, so parity is tick 0; the pool sat at tick 14). No TWAP manipulation moves it.
`test_H1_spreadPushOnStablesIsBoundedByTheAnchor`: a spread push that moves both stables TWAPs 50 ticks gets a range
clipped at -40; one that moves them 100 ticks gets an empty range. The price a Safe trade can be pushed to on
stables is therefore never worse than parity - 40 ticks, about 0.55% from today's price at most, against 0.11% when
nobody manipulates. Liveness: the stables pool stops pricing while USDC/USDT is more than ~0.5% off parity (a
depeg); the owner's path is unaffected.

**Why not a liquidity floor.** The first version of this fix refused when the harmonic-mean in-range liquidity of
either window fell under a floor (a push into an empty range craters it: 3.4% / 0.6% of normal after the stables
9,000-tick block). `test_h1_jitLiquidityAtThePushedTick` shows an attacker defeats it by minting its own narrow
position at the pushed tick for the block boundary: with 100 USDC + 100 USDT of such liquidity the windows keep
79.7% / 39.5% of normal, with 1,000 + 1,000 97.6% / 87.1%, and the TWAP shifts exactly as before. A check that a
returned-capital mint defeats is not worth its liveness cost (LPs leaving would freeze the pool), so it was
removed.

Other alternatives rejected:

- **Consistency check |long - short| <= C alone.** The empty-range rule already is one (C ~ 2 x band); the spread
  push above passes any C.
- **Median of sub-window means.** `observe` at ~30 points (~200-650k gas per check). On stables it only multiplies
  the number of ~25-USDC pushes by ~16.
- **Per-observation clamping.** Needs every observation of the window: ~900 storage reads per check on btc/eth.
- **Cardinality / spacing.** Bounds history length, not what one observation can carry.

### Residual H-1 exposure, stated

- Stables: none beyond the anchor. Worst manipulated execution: anchor - 40 ticks, 0.40% + |price - parity| + fee
  per crossing.
- btc: ~9k USDC of fees and ~10M of own capital at two block boundaries per 1% shift, lasting <= 5 minutes. Per
  account that buys far less than it costs. With the operator compromised (every session key at once) it buys 1%
  of the btc crossings the fleet's buckets allow in those 5 minutes (each bucket is full after a quiet day: W =
  2 x C_btc, 0.8 x the account cap; a mint then a decrease crosses it twice). Break-even: about 0.9M of fleet
  crossings, i.e. **roughly 0.6-1.1M of TVL**, for an attacker who also holds ~10M of its own. Today's accounts are
  capped at 1,000 USDC. Above that size, the fleet-wide on-chain counter (spec open question 5) is needed, or a lower btc
  bucket, or a longer short window.
- WETH/USDC: pushes cost ~0.6% of 18,670 WETH per 1,000 ticks; above any plausible gain.

## The bound, restated

Per crossing, a sandwich takes at most `band + fee + lag`, where `lag` is how far the 5-minute TWAP trails the
market in the adverse direction (the 30-minute one no longer counts: the range uses the safer of the two), plus a
paid TWAP shift (H-1 residual above; on stables capped by the anchor at 0.40% + |price - parity|).
Crossings per pool are at most `V_p + 2 x min(T_p, W_p x (1 + tau / budgetWindow))`, in stable-token value (the
volatile leg is valued at the range edge, so the budget over-counts by up to one band). `tau` is the time from
the leak to the revocation. The token bucket replaces the old "windows touched + 1".

```
L <= sum_p (band_p + fee_p) x (V_p + 2 x min(T_p, W_p x (1 + tau / 1 day)))
```

Agreed parameters (Ot, 18 Oct): W_p = 5 / 2 / 3 x C_p per day, T_p = 10 x C_p, bands 10 / 100 / 100 ticks, fees
1 / 5 / 30 bp, weights 50 / 40 / 10 (`conservadorLiveV4`), V_p = C_p.

| Account cap | Revoked within minutes | Within 1 hour | Within 1 day | Never revoked |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,000 USDC | 36.2 (3.6%) | 37.4 (3.7%) | 66.3 (6.6%) | 127.1 (12.7%) |
| 100,000 USDC | 3,615 (3.6%) | 3,741 (3.7%) | 6,625 (6.6%) | 12,705 (12.7%) |

By pool at 1,000, never revoked: stables 0.11% x 21 x 500 = 11.6; btc 1.05% x 21 x 400 = 88.2; risk 1.30% x 21 x
100 = 27.3. Within a day: stables 11.6 (T binds), btc 1.05% x 9 x 400 = 37.8, risk 1.30% x 13 x 100 = 16.9.

The same accounting on the audited T4 code (per-token budgets, fixed epochs): never revoked 24.8% (V + 4T),
revoked within minutes or a day up to 12.6% (V + 4 x min(T, 2W), at a day boundary).

Not in the table:

- the 5-minute TWAP lag (above);
- a paid TWAP shift (H-1 residual, above). Stables at their anchor edge (about 0.55% per crossing: 0.40% + today's
  0.14% off parity + fee, instead of 0.11%) would add 46 per 1,000 never revoked (17.3% instead of 12.7%);
- userOp gas: without `MamoruGasPolicy`, the account's ETH reserve (0.002 ETH) per relayer top-up. With it, per op
  at most `maxPriorityFeePerGas x gas + gasPrice x (preVerificationGas + 10% of unused callGasLimit)`: at the
  suggested caps (10^7 wei priority, 3M / 2M / 200k gas) and 0.005 gwei base fee, about 5e-5 ETH per op, bounded
  in count by the session's UsageLimitPolicy.

## Liveness changes

- The range is now the intersection of two bands: during a fast move it is narrower than before, and it is empty
  (everything priced refuses) when the two TWAPs are more than about 2 x band apart. T5 measured the 30-minute
  TWAP alone; the two-TWAP refusal rate is not measured.
- The value budget sums both tokens. T5 measured peak days per token (stables USDC 3.68x, USDT 4.85x C_p); on a
  day both legs peak, the sum can exceed W_p = 5 C_p. Not measured; the per-token peaks suggest the stables pool
  is the one to watch.
- The stables anchor stops that pool while USDC/USDT is ~0.5% or more off parity: not measured on 2024-2026 data
  (T5 saw moves up to 73 bp during waits).
- Enable needs `observationCardinalityNext >= 1,800` on every pool: USDC/USDT has 1,000 today. The deploy step
  raises it.
