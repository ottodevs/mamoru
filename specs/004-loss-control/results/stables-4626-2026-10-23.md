# 004 Lab: Conservador stables bucket as USDC supply to an ERC-4626 vault, 23 Oct 2026

Ticket otto/mamoru#186 (proposal from Brais, chat-mamoru, 19 Oct). For Ot and Brais. This is a Lab study only:
`packages/decide` and `apps/*` are unchanged. The LP numbers come from the production `decide` in the simulator. The
vault arm has no engine code behind it (`packages/backtest/src/vault4626.ts`). **Revision 1** (after the GPT-6 Sol
review of 2958a6c, six P1 findings) is at the end, under "Revision after review". The numbers below are the re-run's.
The GO did not change.

**Recommendation: GO for a follow-up adapter ticket** (4626 adapter, session policy, registry entries). The
conditions are in "Recommendation" below. Over the 11 Lab windows the stables bucket kept as single-asset USDC in a
seasoned vault earned more than the USDC/USDT 0.01% LP:

- At 1,000 USDC: +24.8 USDC vs +18.7.
- At 50 USDC: +1.04 vs −4.78.
- The vault came out ahead in 9 of 11 windows.
- The vault never lost more than its two operations' gas. The LP lost up to 10% of a 50 USDC bucket to gas in one
  week.

Every arm here ends in USDC: the LP pays its exit, the vault its redeem. The LP wins in high-volume stable months:
May 2026 gave it +14.7 against +3.6. So the case is steadier income and
fewer operations, not a higher ceiling.

## Question

Today the Conservador stables bucket (`pool:USDC/USDT/100`, ±20 ticks) is an LP position. It re-ranges, harvests and
swaps half its USDC into USDT. The proposal is to replace it with one `deposit` of USDC into an ERC-4626 vault on
Base. Interest would accrue in the share price, with supply only and no borrowing. The questions:

- Is it better net of gas, against holding, at 50 and 1,000 USDC?
- What does it do to the whole mix?
- Which vaults qualify?

## Method

- **LP arm (as today).** The simulator `backtest-1` runs the production `decide` with `conservador-live-v2` and the
  bucket list overridden to **the stables bucket alone at 100%** (`stables-only`). Everything else is the live
  policy: review every sample, sessions enforced. Deposits are 50, 500 and 1,000. Its value is net of its own gas.
  The sweep leaves the position open at the end, so the exit is charged here: a reduce plus a swap of the USDT half
  back, gas at the last sample plus 1 bp on the half, about 0.05–0.15 USDC. **Every comparison uses this terminal
  USDC value**, as every vault pays its redeem. "LP before exit" is kept beside it for traceability.
- **Vault arm.** USDC is deposited at the window's first sample and redeemed at its last (`vaultGrowth`).
  - **Morpho vaults** are scored on the **observed share-price ratio** from the Morpho API (daily, geometric between
    readings). The DefiLlama base-APY model is computed only to report the discrepancy.
  - **waBasUSDC** has no free share-price series, so it is scored on the model: the Aave reserve's daily base rate
    from DefiLlama. That rate is Aave's `liquidityRate`, an **APR**, so it accrues linearly between readings, and
    readings compound with each other as the liquidity index does on each update. The MetaMorpho DefiLlama rate is
    an effective **APY**, net of the vault fee. The static aToken v2 has no fee of its own, so the reserve rate is
    the wrapper's.
  - **Rate readings** apply forward from their own timestamp. Only the base rate counts: rewards are never share
    value, and a day without a base rate is a gap, not zero.
  - **A window is unavailable** when it starts before the vault contract existed (Morpho API for the vaults; the
    Basescan creation tx for waBasUSDC, 20 Jan 2025 18:15 UTC), or when its series does not cover the whole window
    with readings at most 2 days apart (`MAX_GAP_SECONDS`).
  - Each of the two operations is priced with the
  simulator's own gas formula (units × (base fee + 0.001 gwei tip) + 3.1e9 wei L1 fee, in USDC through WETH/USDC) on
  that sample of the window's dataset (`opGasRaw`).
- **Against hold.** The common baseline is holding USDC, which ends at the deposit. The table also gives the LP
  against its own hold (50/50 USDC/USDT).
- **Whole mix at 1,000.** The vault mix is the volatile buckets alone at 500 (cbBTC/WETH 80/20, i.e. 40/10 of the
  account) plus 500 in the vault. It is compared with the live mix (`conservador-live-v2` at 1,000) and with the same
  split done with the LP: volatile 500 plus `stables-only` 500. The gap between "additive LP" and "live" measures the
  error of splitting the mix. Target-weights moves money between buckets, which the split ignores.
- **Windows.** All 13 Lab windows of `windows.ts`. aug2024 has no LP run: the policy's cbBTC caps need a cbBTC price,
  and cbBTC was not on Base yet, the same skip as every other study. sparkUSDC (created 30 Dec 2024) and waBasUSDC
  (20 Jan 2025) predate dec2024 and aug2024, so they are marked n/a there. Nothing is tuned, so TRAIN and TEST are reported side by side, and the split matters only for
  convention.
- **Vault seasoning.** A vault under 10M USD at a window's start is marked `*` but still counted, since its rate is
  what a depositor got then. gtUSDCp was 1–6M from Aug 2024 to Jun 2025, and sparkUSDC was 9.2M at the start of May
  2026.

What this means:

- **The three Morpho vaults are one bet.** Gauntlet, Spark and Steakhouse on Base all hold 91–100% in the same
  cbBTC/USDC 86% LLTV market, and draw on the same 176.6M of withdrawable liquidity. Picking two of them diversifies
  curator and fee, not collateral or liquidity.
- **That collateral is the Conservador's own volatile risk.** The btc-usdc bucket (40%) is already a USDC/cbBTC LP.
  A cbBTC crash or oracle failure hits both buckets at once. Aave's USDC reserve is a different protocol with mixed
  collateral.
- **100% utilization.** At 100% a redeem of any size fails until borrowers repay or the rate curve (up to 4× the
  target rate on Morpho) pulls supply back in. Daily samples hide intraday peaks. For 50–1,000 USDC, any day under
  100% is enough. A failed redeem delays the money but loses none of it.
- **First-depositor inflation is not relevant.** The Morpho vaults each hold over 100M with share prices of
  1.08–1.12, and waBasUSDC wraps a 191M reserve.

## Break-even

Break-even uses the 90-day mean base rate as of today, as an effective APY: gt 4.44%, Spark 3.97%, Aave 3.78%
(its 3.71% APR compounded daily).

At simulator gas, 50 USDC pays its way in **about a week**. At the live relayer's 0.07 $/op it takes **3–4 weeks**,
in line with the ticket's 2–3 weeks. At 1,000 USDC it takes a day or two even at live cost. After that the vault
needs no further operations: no harvest, compound or re-range. Any money the account later adds, such as harvested
fees (#178), is one more deposit of about 0.013–0.07 $ each, so it is worth batching.

## Recommendation

**GO for a follow-up ticket: a 4626 adapter, session policy and registry entries.** The bucket result is:

- More net yield over the Lab windows, with every arm ending in USDC: +24.8 against +18.7 per 1,000, and +1.04
  against −4.78 per 50.
- Far less variance.
- 2 operations instead of 2–34 per window.
- No USDT exposure.

The LP's only advantage is upside in high-volume stable months.

Conditions for the follow-up:

1. **Registry.** Make **gtUSDCp** the primary: oldest, 0% fee today, highest yield in the Lab. Make **waBasUSDC** the
   second, and choose it for protocol and collateral diversification rather than for yield. sparkUSDC, the alternate,
   shares gtUSDCp's market, so it does not diversify. Pin `codeHash` read on-chain, and for waBasUSDC also its
   implementation. Alert on a fee or timelock change.
2. **Session policy.** One target per vault: `deposit(assets, receiver = Safe)` and `redeem(shares, receiver = Safe,
   owner = Safe)` with `EQUAL` on the receiver and owner. Cap the deposit amount by bucket size.
3. **Redeem path.** Check `maxWithdraw` or `maxRedeem` first. When it falls short, the redeem is "retry later", never
   an error that leaves the exit half done. The user's exit must show this case.
4. **Minimum size.** At live relayer cost a vault deposit pays for itself in about a week from roughly 170–200 USDC,
   and in about a month from about 40 USDC. Below that, keep the stables share as idle USDC: it earns nothing and
   costs nothing. Alternatively, accept a break-even of a month or more.
5. **Harvest sink (#178).** Harvested USDC can go to the same vault in batches. Each deposit should be worth at least
   about 20 × its gas.

## Limits

- **The Morpho vaults use their observed share price; waBasUSDC uses a model.** Its model is the reserve's APR,
  because the stata share price is not in a free API. On the Morpho vaults the same kind of model was within 0.06%
  of the deposit per window. Both operations happen at the window's edges. Intraday utilization is not modelled.
- **The LP exit is an estimate** (reduce and swap gas plus 1 bp on the half), charged in every comparison: −0.13 at
  50 and −0.66 at 1,000 over 11 windows. The live mix's exit is approximated by that of a 500 USDC stables position.
- **Gas units are estimates.** The direct receipts are measured. The 350k userOp overhead and the Aave deposit are
  not. Doubling every vault op's units lowers the 11-window totals by about 0.2 USDC.
- **The mix is approximated** by splitting it into volatile and stables halves. That split's own error, −3.8 to +5.2
  per window, is larger than the stables effect in most windows.
- **APY is history.** Today's rates (3.7–4.4%) are below the 2025 windows (5–6%), and any rate below the LP's fee
  yield in busy months shrinks the gap. The windows are short: 7–31 days, 193 days in total.
- **Not done:**
  - `codeHash`, which needs an RPC; it was not read, by the brief's rules.
  - Aave bad-debt history and the stata wrapper's share price.
  - A Euler vault: no Base USDC Euler vault above 2M in DefiLlama today.
  - Fluid fUSDC (8.5M), outside the brief's list.
  - aug2024 LP.
