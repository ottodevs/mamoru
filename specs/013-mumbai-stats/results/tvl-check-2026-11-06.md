# TVL history check, 6 Nov 2026

Question: prod D1 `account_daily` shows Base prod TVL of about 195 USDC on 5 Nov and about 3,772 USDC on 6 Nov. Is the jump real, and does D1 agree with the chain?

Read-only, 6 Nov ~18:10 UTC. D1 `account_daily` and `account_snapshot_latest` (Base, block 53991080); chain reads on Base at block 53993350 (USDC `balanceOf`, and `convertToAssets(balanceOf)` of gtUSDCp and waBasUSDC). Figures are aggregates; no account is named here (STATS-R9).

## Result

- **The jump is real deposits on 6 Nov.** Of the 7 prod accounts holding more than 1 USDC on 6 Nov, 3 were created that day, 1 was created on 5 Nov and funded on the 6th, and 1 older account was topped up from ~106 to ~864 USDC. Their 5 Nov rows are 0 or small, as D1 says.
- **D1 agrees with the chain.** For the three largest accounts (together ~3,410 USDC), the USDC balance on chain equals D1's idle USDC to the cent where idle is all USDC, and the vault assets on chain equal `total − idle − lp − fees − owed` in D1 to within 0.01 USDC. `account_daily.total_close_usdc` for today equals `account_snapshot_latest.total_usdc` for every funded account.
- **Vaults are counted.** In those three accounts the vaults hold ~1,326 USDC, about 39 % of their total.
